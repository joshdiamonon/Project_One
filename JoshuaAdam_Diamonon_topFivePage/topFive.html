Joshua Adam Diamonon's Top Fives!
Top Five Albums


Ariana Grande: "eternal sunshine" The album that I listen to everyday, created by my favorite artists of ALL time. Favorite song: intro (end of the world)
NIKI: "Nicole" Another album that has gotten me though the tough times. Saw her on tour for this album twice and would love to see her again.
Favorite song: Take A Chance With Me
Summer Walker: "Still Over It" In 2021, I hit one of my hardest rock bottoms mentally, even dropping out of SF State at the time, and this album truly saved my life at the time.
Favorite song: Session 33

Thuy: "wings" This album is more a comfort album for me, but also a testimate for Thuy's career. Growing from small artist from the Bay Area, to successful artist in the Industry.
Favorite song: Cloud 11
Kehlani "While We Wait" An album that defined my high school years, listening to it everyday on the way to school, during school, and on the way home from school.
Favorite song: Footsteps
