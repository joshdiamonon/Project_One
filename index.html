Joshua Adam Diamonon


22 years old, LGBTQ Filipino American, born and raised in the Bay Area.
Filled with passion for all things music, art, photography, video games design and much more. Currently studying Visual Communication Design and Video Game Studies at San Francisco State University.
Born as a second generation Filipino American and growing up in Daly City, I have always felt the need to break out of the fog that encapsulates our city to find what brings me joy in life. I fell in love with photography in high school, so much so, I became president of yearbook my senior year. Designing my school's yearbook was my first real design project and was definately challenging my creativity. As time for committing to college, I was unsure about everything and originally declared as a Business Administration major at San Francisco University. After moving out alone at eighteen, I found myself and my passion for design as I tackled challenges head on. Through interior design for decorating my home, to creating systems to manage inventory to ease workload at my jobs, to even the entertainment I consume such as drag and video games, I fell in love with design and all aspects of it I could pursue.

To me, design brings out freedom and creativity that I so desperately wanted to find yet, it was in me the entire time, I just didn't know it yet.
