Who is Jawshy?

Real name, Joshua Adam Diamonon,
22 years old, LGBTQ Filipino American, born and raised in the Bay Area. Filled with passion for all things music, art, photography, video games and much more. Currently studying Visual Communication Design and Video Game Studies at San Francisco State University.

Born as a second generation Filipino American and growing up in Daly City, Josh has always felt the need to break out of the fog that encapsulates the city to find what brings him joy in life. Josh fell in love with photography in high school, becoming president of yearbook his senior year, and tackled his first real design project. Designing his school's yearbook was Josh's first real design project and was definately challenging to his creativity and design process. After moving out alone at eighteen, he found himself and his passion for design as he tackled challenges head on. Through interior design for decorating his home, to creating systems to manage inventory to ease workload at his jobs, to even the entertainment Josh consumed such as drag and video games that inspired him creatively, Josh fell in love with design and all aspects of it he could pursue.
"Design brings out freedom and creativity that I so desperately wanted to find yet, it was in me the entire time, I just didn't know it yet."
- Joshua Adam Diamonon
